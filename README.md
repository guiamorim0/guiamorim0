### JOVI CAM

Academic project for the JOVI Smartphone Challenge 2026 (FIAP and JOVI Mobile), developed by the Pentacode group, a team of 5.

Smart camera app for students with an AI Mode (detects the scene and adjusts the camera) and a Study Mode (captures the whiteboard, transcribes the text and generates summaries).

- Qualified for Sprint 4 among 113 groups and the only group in the class to advance
- Validated with 12+ users: 100% felt an improvement in their study performance

**Stack:** Python, Gemini API, React
